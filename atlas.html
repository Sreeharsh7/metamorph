Dataset

One row per structure pair. The pair list and fold-switching regions are from Chakravarty & Porter (2022). Of their 93 pairs, 92 are included; SarA (PDB 1FZP) has been removed from the PDB. Structures and annotations come from the RCSB PDB.

Methods

Pairing. For each pair, Cα coordinates of the listed chains (first model, first alternate location) are retrieved from the RCSB ModelServer and aligned by global sequence alignment with free end gaps. Only aligned residues are used. The published fold-switching region is located by its sequence, or by residue range where the sequence is not found.

Secondary structure. Assigned from Cα geometry alone, using distance windows for helices and extended strands with a partner-strand check (in the spirit of P-SEA). It is recomputed for every frame of the morph, which is why the viewer can show a helix unwinding. It will differ from DSSP in places.

Morph. The two structures are superposed with an iterative Gaussian-weighted fit, so the conserved core aligns. The path is built by targeted relaxation: each Cα moves at most 0.35 Å per step toward fold B while virtual bonds, i/i+2 distances and excluded volume are enforced. If this stalls, moving segments are routed around the core or locally unfolded. For 90 of the 92 pairs no frame brings the chain closer to itself than the real structures do. Two pairs (3JV6/1ZK9 and 3O44/1XEZ) are marked as approximate.

Limitations. The morph is a geometric illustration of an allowed path, not a simulation of the real transition, which may involve unfolding, oligomerisation or binding partners. Some pairs compare a monomer with a chain taken from a complex or assembly.
